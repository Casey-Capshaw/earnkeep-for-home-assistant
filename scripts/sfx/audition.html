The escalation scheme being proposed

  • Per kid. Each kid has their own combo, so one kid's streak doesn't lift another's sound.
  • Window: 10 seconds since that kid's last check (try the slider above). Every check restarts the clock.
  • Levels 1 to 5. Each check in the window moves up one level: higher notes, then fairy dust at 3, a warm chord underneath at 4, and the full run with answering notes at 5. Past 5 it stays at 5.
  • Habits and paid tasks share the combo. A paid task plays the richer strum-and-coins sound at whatever level the kid is on.
  • Finales. Last habit on the board done: the habits finale (same moment as the confetti). Week's paycheck full: the coin-shower finale.
  • Undo plays a small soft step down, takes that item back out of the combo, and doesn't restart the clock. Re-checking the same tile can't climb the ladder twice.
  • Bank deposit (from Admin) gets coins-into-the-jar. Withdrawals and the automatic paycheck/interest stay silent.
